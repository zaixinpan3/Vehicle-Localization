# Road support before fine curb continuation

## User observation and diagnosis

In Mississippi frame 943, the user identified every displayed left-curb detection as a false positive and identified the true right-curb vicinity around the 86 points recorded in `tests/reference/curbBoundaryVicinity943.json`. Other displayed right-curb branches were false positives. The baseline, commit b42d7516f399fa9e668eb7658311248c18e487e3, produced 212 curb points.

Tracing the initial refinement found separate models around left y=6 m and right y=-2.58, -2.90, -3.66 and -4.03 m. Incorrect primary models also seeded further false continuations. Requiring both side surfaces to share a smooth plane would reject some previously accepted curbs beside rough elevated terrain, including frame 1137. Independent side fits instead showed a useful distinction: frame-943 left primary boundaries had median lower-surface residuals of 0.0443 and 0.0587 m, while the two true right boundaries had residuals of 0.00711 and 0.00516 m. The false right branches had residuals of 0.01527, 0.03032 and 0.01380 m and lay above nearby, parallel true boundaries.

## Implementation

`validateCurbRoadSupport` validates completed fine boundary models before they seed continuation. Within existing 0.65 m XY neighborhoods, it excludes the central 0.12 m half-width strip and fits independent planes on the two observed sides, each with at least six points and a nonsingular design. It compares their intercepts at the boundary position and measures the residual of the lower surface. At least three occupied proposal cells must provide measurements before a boundary can be rejected; sparse inconclusive cases remain. A median lower-surface residual above 0.035 m rejects a model. Roughness of the elevated side alone does not reject a curb.

Among supported boundaries, a raised parallel model is rejected only when a majority of its points have nearby lower-boundary witnesses, the lower boundary has at least 1.5 times better road-surface residual, and the conflict occupies at least three XY cells. Witnesses must satisfy the existing metric tangent, separation and neighborhood constraints and lie more than half the minimum curb relief below the compared return. A numerical residual floor prevents perfect planar fits from competing on roundoff. This comparison acts on already supported models, not an assumed left/right side or fixed road width.

`refineCurbGeometry` now derives its accepted original-point mask from the validated boundary lists, including recovered boundaries. The redundant recovered-point accumulator was removed. Coarse processing remains whole-pillar analysis; inference uses unorganized metric XYZ with no frame, point-index or ring conditions.
